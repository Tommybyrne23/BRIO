## Archetype — Sage 70 / Ruler 30
 
**Sage (primary).** Drawn from the founding documents, which refuse to overclaim in
five separate places: evidence and uncertainty shown with every recommendation;
within-person baselines instead of universal HRV thresholds; the 2022 meta-analysis
cited *against* Brio's own coaching claim; the positioning labelled a hypothesis; a
competitor's flattering "gaps" article treated as leads to verify; agent dissent
published in the explain card. None of those were brand decisions, which is why the
archetype is credible.
 
**Ruler (secondary).** Block → split → movement → set → load × reps × RPE. Exactly five
permitted outputs. Signal-level consent, source attribution, audit log, export and
delete. The qualification that keeps it from paternalism: **Brio rules the data, never
the athlete.** Every recommendation is editable; every signal can be switched off.
 
Both sit on the Order half of the archetype map, which is why the blend holds.
 
**Rejected, with reasons:**
- **Hero** — the category default, so invisible; and the delivery plan's own risk
  register says no performance claim is defensible. Hero writes cheques the evidence
  can't cash.
- **Magician** — sells the mystery, hides the method. The USP is the audit trail.
- **Caregiver** — closest miss. Its failure mode is paternalism, which collides with the
  editable recommendation. Care in Brio appears as governance, not nurture.
**The ratio in practice:** ~7 sentences in 10 explain, qualify or evidence; ~3 structure,
bound or record. Structure without reasoning = a dashboard. Reasoning without a bounded
action = an essay.
 
## Voice — four principles
 
1. **Show the working.** Evidence and uncertainty in the same breath.
2. **Bound the claim.** "Above your own 7-day baseline", not "not recovered".
3. **The lifter decides.** Recommend, never instruct. No streaks, no guilt, no praise.
4. **Plain over clinical, precise over casual.**
**The test:** could a sceptical strength coach read this sentence and check it?
 
**Banned:** crush · beast mode · grind · unlock · transform · journey · effortless ·
revolutionary · game-changing · AI-powered · seamlessly · optimise (bare) · personalised
(unqualified) · smart · "62% recovered".

**Three rules:**
1. Sage is a field, never a sentence — 2.85:1 on Bone. Type on Sage is Ink only.
2. On Mist, component boundaries are Bríoch, not Sage (Sage/Mist is 2.60:1, under the
   3:1 floor for interface components).
3. Hue never carries a state. Under deuteranopia Bronze, Rust and Slate converge, so
   every recommendation ships with its word.
**Bounded actions:** Progress = Sage · Maintain = Slate · Repeat = Bríoch ·
Reduce = Bronze · Escalate = Rust.
 
## Logo
 
Triquetra inside a ring, all circular arcs, interlaced. **Three petals = the three
signals** (training, recovery, nutrition). **Ring = the loop** (connect, log, compare,
decide, learn). Woven rather than stacked, because the claim is that the three are
inseparable.
 
Greg's original equities kept: the sage green and the triquetra-as-the-o. Changed:
the mark now sits at x-height so the word reads before the symbol; strand weight matches
the letterform stems; the tittle is a plain dot so the logo carries one knot, not two.
The quatrefoil is retained as a separate ornament asset.
 
- Wordmark: **Spectral Medium**, outlined. UI/data: **Inter**.
- Clear space = the x-height of the wordmark on all four sides.
- Full mark: never below 32 px / 10 mm. Below that use the compact mark (ring dropped,
  strand thickened) — legible to 16 px.
## Naming
 
**brí** — Irish: *strength, vigour; force, significance*, from Proto-Celtic *\*brīgos*.
One word meaning both **energy** and **meaning** — the exact join the product makes.
Italian *con brio* is the second reading. The name authenticates the Celtic mark rather
than decorating with it.
